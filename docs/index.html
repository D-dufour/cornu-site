01 — The system

A continuously evolving understanding of the world.

Sensors observe. Edge compute interprets. External data adds what sensors cannot see. History adds what the moment cannot show. Cornu resolves all of it into one representation a machine can act on.

Inputs
CameraRadarAIS PositionTelemetryEnvironment Charts & noticesPrior observations
Constraint

No single sensor describes a waterway. A camera loses the channel at night, radar cannot read a bridge sign, AIS only reports vessels that transmit. Fusion is not a feature of the product — it is the product.

→ 01

Perception

Detection, segmentation and tracking across fused sensor streams. Vessels, infrastructure, markers, debris, shoreline.

→ 02

Spatial understanding

Observations become geometry: positions, distances, clearances, channel boundaries and navigable space in a consistent frame.

→ 03

World model

Geometry becomes a persistent representation — what is here, what changed since the last passage, what conditions apply now.

→ 04

Intelligence

Prediction and reasoning over that representation: trajectories, conflicts, lock and bridge timing, route risk.

→ 05

Autonomy

The same layer that informs a crew can inform a control system. Assistance first, automation as it is earned.

07 — Architecture

One stack, from sensor to decision.

Physical

CamerasRadarPositioningTelemetryEnvironmental sensing

Marine-grade enclosures and an onboard compute unit sized for continuous operation. Installed per vessel, calibrated per hull.

Perception

Object understandingGeometryMotionSensor fusion

Models trained on inland waterway conditions rather than road scenes — reflection, glare, fog, night operation, low-contrast water surfaces.

World model

Spatial intelligenceHistorical contextEnvironment representationPrediction

A shared representation that persists between passages and between vessels, updated as observations arrive and reconciled against charts, water levels and notices.

Intelligence

NavigationSafetyFleet intelligenceAutonomy

Warnings, clearance and timing support, route intelligence and fleet-level analytics — delivered onboard and to shore.

Applications

MaritimeRoboticsMobilityInfrastructurePhysical AI

Maritime products sit on top of a general architecture. Other domains reuse the layers beneath rather than starting again.
