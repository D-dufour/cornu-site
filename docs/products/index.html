Bridge Watch.

Know your clearance.
Before the approach.

Bridge Watch measures the opening ahead against your vessel’s air draft, helping the crew recognise clearance concerns earlier and make a more informed approach.

For inland vessels / Pilot deployments

Why it matters

Protect your vessel.
Keep voyages moving.

A clearance mistake can damage equipment, interrupt a voyage and take a vessel out of service. Bridge Watch gives the crew a measured margin to support earlier, better-informed decisions.

More time to respond.

Flag a narrowing margin early, giving the crew more time to assess the approach and act before a clearance concern becomes urgent.

Less avoidable disruption.

Earlier awareness can help reduce the risk of a clearance mistake and the repairs, downtime or schedule changes that could follow.

Another reference for the crew.

Add a vessel-specific measurement to charts, visual checks and experience, especially when the route or conditions are less familiar.

How it works

One clear view.
Your vessel. Your margin.

Mast-mounted sensing and onboard compute, calibrated to your vessel. The measured opening becomes a margin the crew can use.
